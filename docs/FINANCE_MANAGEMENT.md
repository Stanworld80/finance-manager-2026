# The Envelope Methodology - Finance Management Guide

Finance Manager 2026 isn't just a ledger; it's a tool designed around the **Envelope Method**. This guide explains the philosophy behind the app and how to use it for financial freedom.

## 1. What is the Envelope Method?
Imagine you get your salary in cash. You take several paper envelopes and write categories on them: "Rent", "Electricity", "Groceries", "Fun". You put the exact amount of cash needed into each envelope.

- When you go to the store, you only take money from the "Groceries" envelope.
- If it's empty, you can't buy more food unless you choose to move money from the "Fun" envelope.
- **Rule**: If it's not in an envelope, you can't spend it.

## 2. Real Accounts vs. Virtual Envelopes
In the modern world, your money is in a bank, not in paper envelopes.

- **The Real Account** is the container (e.g., your Boursorama account).
- **The Envelopes** are labels we put on that money inside the container.

**The Golden Equation:**  
`Bank Balance = Sum of all Envelopes`

Every Euro you own must have a "job" (be in an envelope). If you don't know what it's for, it belongs in the **"Libre" (Free)** envelope.

## 3. The "Engaged" (Engagé) State
One common pitfall in budgeting is forgetting that a payment hasn't left the bank yet.

When you pay by credit card:
1. The money is **Engaged**: It's no longer in your "Groceries" budget.
2. But it's still in your **Bank Account** (as a pending transaction).

The app handles this by moving the money to a technical state called "Engagé". This ensures your "Available to Spend" amount is always accurate, even before the bank updates.

## 4. How to Handle Unforeseen Expenses
Life happens. If your car breaks down and you don't have a "Car Repair" envelope, you have two choices:
- **Move money**: Transfer from "Loisirs" or "Vacances" to cover it.
- **Use the Free Balance**: Take from the "Libre" envelope.

## 5. Income Distribution
When your salary arrives, it goes into "À Distribuer". This is your "waiting room".
Don't let it sit there! Immediately distribute it to your various budgets based on your priorities for the month.
