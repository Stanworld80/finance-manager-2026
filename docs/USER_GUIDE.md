# Finance Manager 2026 - User Guide

This guide helps you master the features of Finance Manager 2026 to take full control of your financial life.

## Key Concepts

- **Real Accounts**: These are your actual bank accounts (Checking, Savings, PayPal, etc.). They reflect the reality of your bank statements.
- **Envelopes (Virtual Accounts)**: These are logical subdivisions of your Real Accounts. They represent your budgets or specific goals (e.g., "Rent", "Groceries", "New Car Fund").
- **Double-Entry Logic**: Every transaction moves money from one place to another. If you spend 50€ on groceries, 50€ moves from your "Groceries" envelope to the "Spends" (Engaged) state.

---

## 1. The Dashboard
Your financial cockpit. It displays:
- **Global Balance**: Total money available across all real accounts.
- **Recent Activity**: The last transactions you've made.
- **Upcoming Recurrences**: Payments and incomes scheduled for the near future.

## 2. Managing Transactions
To add a transaction, use the colorful shortcut buttons in the top bar:
- **Revenue (Green)**: Money coming in. You must specify which *Real Account* it hits and which *Envelope* it fills.
- **Expense (Red)**: Money going out. Select the *Real Account* and the *Envelope* that covers the cost.
- **Transfer (Blue)**: Moving money between two *Envelopes*. This doesn't affect your bank balance, only your internal organization.

### Pro Tip: Split Transactions
If a single receipt (e.g., a 100€ supermarket bill) covers multiple budgets, you can "Split" the transaction to allocate 70€ to "Groceries" and 30€ to "Home Maintenance".

## 3. Recurring Transactions
Stop entering the same data every month!
- Go to the **Échéanciers** section.
- Add your rent, subscriptions, and salary.
- Set the frequency and start date. The app will automatically project these into your future balance.

## 4. Bank Import (CSV)
For those who prefer bulk updates:
- Export your bank statement as a CSV.
- Go to **Import CSV**.
- Upload the file. The app will attempt to recognize dates and amounts.
- Review and "Map" the transactions to your envelopes before final confirmation.

## 5. Projects & Goals
Saving for a vacation?
- Create a **Project** in the dashboard.
- Set a target amount and date.
- Link an envelope to this project to track your progress visually.
